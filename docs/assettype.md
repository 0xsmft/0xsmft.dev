# AssetType

8-bit enum.

## Valid values

| Name  | Numerical Value | File extension |
| ------| ------| ---- |
| Texture | 0 (0x0) | .stx |
| StaticMesh | 1 (0x1) | .gsnd |
| SkeletalMesh | 2 (0x2) | .scene |
| Material | 3 (0x3) | .smaterial
| MaterialInstance | 4 (0x4) | N/A
| Sound | 5 (0x5) | .snd
| GraphSound | 6 (0x6) | .gsnd
| Scene | 7 (0x7) | .scene
| Prefab | 8 (0x8) | .prefab
| Skeleton | 9 (0x9) | .skel
| PhysicsMaterial | 10 (0xA) | .sphysmaterial
| BehaviourTree | 11 (0xB) | .sbt
| BehaviourTreeMemory | 12 (0xC) | .sbtm
| SkeletalAnimation | 13 (0xD) | .skanim
| AnimationController | 14 (0xE) | .sac
| Font | 15 (0xF) | .saf
| StyleProfile | 16 (0x10) | .ssp
| PhysSurfaceRegistry | 17 (0x11) | .spsr
| Unknown | 18 (0x12) | N/A
